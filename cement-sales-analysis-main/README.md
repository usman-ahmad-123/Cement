# Cement Sales Analytics & End-to-End ETL Pipeline

## Project Overview

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing monthly cement sales from **2010 to 2022**. The project combines data engineering, statistical analysis, machine learning, time-series forecasting, SQL, and business intelligence to understand sales behavior and operational performance.

A complete **ETL workflow** was implemented to transform raw cement-industry data into a structured SQLite database. The cleaned data was subsequently used for exploratory analysis, KPI development, economic-driver analysis, sales forecasting, and interactive Power BI reporting.

The project demonstrates practical experience in **Python, SQL, data preprocessing, statistical modeling, machine learning, forecasting, and dashboard development**.

# Analytical Workflow

## 1. Data Engineering & ETL

A Python-based ETL workflow was created to convert the raw dataset into a structured analytical data source.

### Extract

* Imported the original CSV dataset using Pandas.
* Examined data types, missing records, duplicate entries, and inconsistent values.

### Transform

* Standardized column formats.
* Processed missing and inconsistent observations.
* Converted variables into suitable analytical data types.
* Prepared the dataset for statistical and SQL analysis.

### Load

* Exported the cleaned dataset as CSV.
* Stored the transformed data in a **SQLite database** for downstream analysis.

Generated database:

```text
data/processed/cement_factory.db
```

---

## 2. Exploratory Data Analysis

The cleaned dataset was explored using **Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn** to understand the underlying sales and production behavior.

The analysis focused on:

* Long-term movement in cement sales.
* Monthly sales variations.
* Seasonal demand behavior.
* Production versus demand.
* Year-over-year performance.
* Relationships between sales and macroeconomic indicators.

Visual analysis was used to identify patterns that could later support KPI development and forecasting.

---

## 3. Operational KPI Analysis

SQL was used to convert the processed data into business-oriented performance metrics.

The analysis included:

### Production Efficiency

Measures how effectively production capacity is utilized relative to the relevant demand or operational benchmark.

### Demand Fulfillment

Evaluates the organization's ability to satisfy market demand through available production and sales.

### Production Gap

Identifies differences between production levels and market demand.

### Annual Sales Performance

Compares sales performance across different years to identify growth and decline periods.

### Low-Efficiency Analysis

Highlights periods where operational efficiency falls below the selected performance threshold.

The resulting analytical datasets are stored under:

```text
data/query_results/
```

---

## 4. Economic Driver Analysis

To investigate the relationship between cement sales and macroeconomic conditions, **Ridge Regression** was implemented using Scikit-learn.

The model considered variables such as:

* GDP
* Interest rate
* Population
* Other available economic indicators

Regularized regression was selected to provide a more stable estimation when multiple explanatory variables are used simultaneously.

The analysis was used to determine which economic variables showed stronger associations with changes in cement sales.

---

## 5. Time-Series Forecasting

A **Prophet-based forecasting model** was developed to estimate cement sales for the subsequent 12 months.

The forecasting workflow included:

1. Preparing historical sales observations.
2. Structuring the data into the required time-series format.
3. Training the Prophet model on historical observations.
4. Capturing long-term trends and recurring seasonal behavior.
5. Generating a 12-month forecast.
6. Comparing predicted values with historical sales patterns.

The forecast indicated approximately **5–8% projected sales growth** for the following year under the assumptions of the model.

# Key Business Insights

The analysis generated several notable observations:

* Cement sales exhibited an overall **upward long-term trend** throughout the analyzed period.
* Monthly data revealed **recurring seasonal variations**, with stronger sales generally observed during the middle portion of the year.
* Production efficiency and demand fulfillment were generally maintained at **above 95%**, indicating comparatively strong operational performance.
* **GDP showed a strong association with cement sales**, supporting the connection between broader economic activity and construction-material demand.
* The forecasting model indicated approximately **5–8% potential year-ahead sales growth**, assuming historical patterns and modeled conditions remain broadly consistent.
